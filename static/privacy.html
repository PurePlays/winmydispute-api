Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 23, 2026

1. Introduction

WinMyDispute GPT provides dispute analysis and premium dispute-kit generation through a custom GPT and supporting API infrastructure. This policy explains what information we process, why we process it, and the third parties involved in delivery of the service.

2. Information We Process

  • Dispute intake details: facts you provide about the merchant, charge, timeline, and supporting evidence.
  • Email address: collected only when needed for checkout creation or license lookup.
  • Payment data: processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Operational metadata: request IDs, limited logs, and abuse-prevention telemetry needed to operate and secure the API.
  • License records: email-based entitlement records stored in our license file for premium access control.

3. How We Use Information

  • To deliver free preview analysis and premium dispute-kit generation.
  • To create Stripe checkout sessions and confirm payment fulfillment.
  • To verify whether an email address has premium access.
  • To maintain reliability, security, fraud prevention, and debugging of the API.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. Third Parties

We share information only with service providers required to operate the product, such as:

  • Stripe for payment processing and checkout fulfillment.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers for API delivery and availability.
  • Optional Google Sheets sync if enabled for internal operational logging of paid licenses.

5. Data Retention

Premium entitlement is stored by email so a paid user can return to the same GPT flow later and be recognized immediately. Operational logs and request metadata may be retained for security, debugging, and financial recordkeeping for a limited period consistent with business and legal needs.

6. Security

We use bearer-authenticated API access, Stripe webhook signature verification, basic rate limiting, and request tracing to protect the service. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, but we work to minimize unnecessary data collection and exposure.

7. Your Rights

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we control, contact support@winthisdispute.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@winthisdispute.com.

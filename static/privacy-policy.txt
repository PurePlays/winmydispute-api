Privacy Policy for WinMyDispute GPT

Last updated: March 23, 2026

WinMyDispute GPT processes dispute-intake information to provide free preview analysis and paid premium dispute-kit generation.

We collect and process:
- dispute facts the user provides
- email addresses when needed for checkout creation or license lookup
- payment events handled by Stripe
- limited operational metadata such as request IDs and abuse-prevention logs
- email-based premium license records

We do not store full payment card details on our servers.

We use this information to:
- deliver preview and premium dispute outputs
- create Stripe checkout sessions
- verify paid entitlement by email
- secure, maintain, and debug the API

Third parties may include Stripe, hosting providers, and optionally Google Sheets if operational sync is enabled.

For privacy requests or questions, contact: support@winthisdispute.com
